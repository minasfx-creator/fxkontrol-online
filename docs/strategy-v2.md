# FXKONTROL Integrated Strategy Command Hub

## Positioning
**The Operating System for Massive Spectacles** — US premium market.

Three commercial messages:
1. **End the broken stage** — replace fragmented drone, pyro, DMX, approval and reporting tools with one command surface.
2. **Sell before deployment** — SkyCanvas, Unreal/Pixel Streaming, AR Overlay and reports before hardware mobilization.
3. **Safety-first spectacle OS** — simulation, evidence, readiness, rollback and audit as sales proof.

## Product Pillars
- **Strategic Command Hub** — GTM, asset library, AI choreography, DockTwin, client approval. Exports strategy JSON. Never executes commands.
- **Go-Live Center** — source of operational trust. Field/hardware claims require evidence, rollback, signoff and readiness.
- **AI Choreography Studio** — SwarmGPT evolved. Generates editable scenes, drone formations, DMX looks, visual timing. Must NOT generate chemical recipes, manufacturing or unsafe firing instructions.
- **DockTwin Pilot** — physical-digital twin (XLR control language, mobile, modular peripherals, bench validation). Pilot until evidence + service flow + telemetry + compliance review complete.
- **Client Approval Flow** — visual and contractual. Real commands stay outside.

## Claim Policy
- `validated` — safe to use as factual or source-backed.
- `pilot` — usable with pilot language and disclaimers.
- `marketing_hypothesis` — useful for narrative, not proof.

NFPA, FAA, latency, cost-reduction, range and hardware claims require careful US review before commercial guarantees.

## 90-Day Execution
**Days 1–15** — Inventory assets · classify by audience/funnel/language · first US pitch package · map module maturity · connect narrative to Go-Live and product docs.

**Days 16–35** — Use Hub in live demos · segmented landing + sales follow-up · client approval reports · export strategy reports per demo.

**Days 36–60** — AI Choreography Studio with real validation + Skybrush export binding · DockTwin live companion telemetry (bench) · evidence on DockTwin + Go-Live readiness.

**Days 61–90** — Three US-facing pilot demos · collect objections + pricing feedback · package Previs / LiveOps Pilot / Enterprise · convert one pilot into documented case.
